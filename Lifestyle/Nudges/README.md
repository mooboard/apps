# Nudges

Gentle reminders to drink water and to switch between sitting and standing.

A glass empties and a figure sits or stands. When time is up, the glass pulses and the figure bobs. Press I drank or I switched in your board's web app, and the timer starts over.

## Settings

- Nudges: both, water, or stand.
- Water every: 30 minutes to 2 hours.
- Switch every: 20 minutes to 90 minutes.
- Quiet hours: off, a set night, or whenever the board is in night mode.

Nothing leaves your board.
