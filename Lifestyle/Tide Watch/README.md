# Tide Watch

The tide where you live, rising or falling, and when the next high and low come.

The sea level comes from the Open-Meteo marine forecast, with no key. Your board finds the next high and low tide in it and shows their times on its own clock. It's a guide for a walk on the beach, not for boating.

Your place is your board's, the one you picked at setup. Away from the sea it says so.

## Settings

- Tide curve adds a second page with the next day's tides as a curve.
