# OK to Wake

A sleep light that tells kids when it's time to get up.

From bedtime, a dim amber moon and stars glow all night, even during rest hours. At wake time, a bright sun and "Good morning" stay up for 30 minutes. During the day, it shows tonight's wake time.

It needs the time, so it waits until your board's clock is set. Its rotation switch only hides the daytime card. Remove the app to turn off the night light.

## Settings

- Bedtime: 7:00 to 9:00 PM, on the half hour.
- Wake time: 6:00 to 8:00 AM, on the half hour.
- Show clock: off by default.

Change its settings in your board's web app.
