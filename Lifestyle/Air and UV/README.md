# Air and UV

Air quality and UV index for your place, each in a color for its level.

Both come from Open-Meteo, with no key. Air quality is the US index, from good to hazard. UV runs from low to extreme.

Your place is your board's, the one you picked at setup. Change it in your board's web app.

## Complications

- Air quality and UV index for your clock faces, each beside a bar in its level's color.
