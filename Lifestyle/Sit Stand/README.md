# Sit Stand

A calm reminder to switch between sitting and standing. When it's time, a figure gets up from its chair, or sits back down if you're standing.

Press I switched in your board's web app and the timer starts over. Press Snooze 10 min for a little longer.

## Settings

- Every 20 minutes to 90 minutes.
- Active hours, all day or a set part of it. The first reminder comes a full interval after they start.
- Skip at night, so reminders wait while your board rests.
- Gentle or bold, for a soft or a bright reminder.

Nothing leaves your board.
