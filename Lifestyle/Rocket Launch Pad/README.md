# Rocket Launch Pad

The next rocket launch with its countdown, and a liftoff on your board when it goes.

Launches come from The Space Devs' Launch Library, with no key, every half hour. The countdown runs on your board's clock. When a launch only has a month or a day so far, it shows the date instead.

## Settings

- Launches from picks every launch in the world or only those from the United States.
