# Water Reminder

A calm reminder to drink water. Between reminders a glass slowly empties, and when it's time, it fills again.

Press I drank in your board's web app and the timer starts over. Press Snooze 10 min for a little longer.

## Settings

- Every 20 minutes to 2 hours.
- Active hours, all day or a set part of it. The first reminder comes a full interval after they start.
- Skip at night, so reminders wait while your board rests.
- Gentle or bold, for a soft or a bright reminder.

Nothing leaves your board.
