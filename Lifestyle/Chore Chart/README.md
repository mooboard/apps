# Chore Chart

Whose turn it is for each chore, tapped done on your phone.

Add up to six chores and up to four people. The turn moves to the next person every day or every week. Today's open chores glow until someone taps them done.

## Settings

- People: up to four names, separated by commas.
- Rotate: daily or weekly. Weeks start on Monday.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Add chores and mark them done in your board's web app.
