# Fantasy Football

Your Sleeper fantasy football matchup this week, with both scores live during games.

Your team is on top and theirs is below, with a bar for each side's share of the points. LIVE shows while the scores move, and FINAL once the week is done. With more than one league, they take turns.

Your board asks api.mooboard.co, which reads Sleeper's public data. No password or key is needed, and nothing is stored.

## Settings

- Sleeper username.
- League, All or a part of its name.
