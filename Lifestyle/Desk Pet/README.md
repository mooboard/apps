# Desk Pet

A tiny pet lives on your board. It wanders, blinks, and stops to look around. After 6 hours it gets hungry and shows you its bowl, and it naps when your board's night begins.

Nothing leaves your board.

## Settings

- Color is your pet's color.
- Sleeps at night lets it nap through your board's night.

## Controls

- Feed fills its bowl, and it comes to eat.
- Pet sends up hearts.
- Mood shows how it's doing.
