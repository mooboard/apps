# Desk Sprout

A little sprout in a pot keeps time for your real plant. It stands tall after you water, droops and yellows as the day comes, and asks when it's time. It grows a new pair of leaves each week you keep it.

Nothing leaves your board.

## Settings

- Water every sets the days between waterings, from 2 days to 2 weeks.
- Pot is the pot's color.

## Controls

- I watered it starts the count over, and the sprout perks up.
- Next watering shows when it's due.
