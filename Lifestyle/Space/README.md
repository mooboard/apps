# Space

When the space station passes over you, the next meteor shower, and the next eclipse.

The station's position comes from wheretheiss.at, with no key. Your board works out when it next comes within sight of you, to within a few minutes. Meteor showers and eclipses come from a list built into the app.

## Settings

- Latitude and longitude of your place.
- Space station, meteor showers, and eclipses can each be turned off.
