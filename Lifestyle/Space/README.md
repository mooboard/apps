# Space

When the space station passes over you, the next meteor shower, the next eclipse you can see, and tonight's moon.

The station's position comes from wheretheiss.at, with no key. Your board works out when it next comes within sight of you, to within a few minutes. Meteor showers and eclipses come from a list built into the app, and it shows the next eclipse seen from your place. Tonight's moon is worked out on your board, with how much of it is lit.

Your place is your board's, the one you picked at setup.

## Settings

- Space station, meteor showers, eclipses, and tonight's moon can each be turned off.
