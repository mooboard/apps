# Box Breathing

A calm square to breathe by. A dot walks one side of the square for each part of the breath, and the square fills as you breathe in and empties as you breathe out. The big number counts down the seconds.

Nothing leaves your board.

## Settings

- Pattern is Box (4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold), Relax (4 in, 7 hold, 8 out), or Even (5 in, 5 out).
- Color is the square's color.

## Controls

- Start over begins a new round from breathing in.
- Rounds counts the rounds since you opened it.
