# Aurora Watch

The planetary Kp index now and tonight, from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center, with no key. An aurora over the hills grows with tonight's Kp. Your board works out the Kp your place needs to see it on the horizon, and says to look north (or south) when tonight should reach it.

Your place is your board's, the one you picked at setup. The Kp you need is an estimate, and dark skies away from city lights help.

## Readouts

- Kp now, Tonight, and Kp you need.
