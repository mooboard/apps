# Med Reminder

A reminder for your daily doses. When one is due, the bottle opens and a pill hops out until you say you took it. A dot for each dose fills as you take them, and the count starts over each day.

It never asks what you take, and nothing leaves your board.

## Settings

- Doses is once, twice, or three times a day. Twice is 12 hours apart, and three times is 6 hours apart.
- First dose is the time of the day's first one.

## Controls

- I took it counts a dose.
- Next dose and Today show what's next and how many you've taken.
