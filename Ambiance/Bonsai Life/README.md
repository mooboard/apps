# Bonsai Life

A bonsai in its pot through the year. Blossoms and petals drift in spring, the pads turn deep green in summer, orange leaves fall in the fall, and snow settles on bare branches in winter. The pads sway a little, and fireflies come out on summer nights.

Nothing leaves your board.

## Settings

- Season follows the date, flipped south of the equator, or stays on the one you pick.
- Pot is the pot's color.
