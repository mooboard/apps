# Oscilloscope

A phosphor scope that sweeps waves and traces spinning figures, with a soft afterglow.

The beam sweeps across the screen and leaves a fading trail, as an old tube does. Waves change shape every few seconds, and the figures turn slowly as their phase drifts.

## Settings

- Trace shows waves, figures, or both taking turns.
- Phosphor picks green, amber, blue, or white.
- Grid turns the scope's grid on or off.
