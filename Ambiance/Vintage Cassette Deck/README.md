# Vintage Cassette Deck

A mixtape plays in its deck, reels turning, meters dancing, and the tape flipping to side B.

The tape winds from one reel to the other in real time, so a side lasts as long as the tape. Then it turns over and plays the other side.

## Settings

- Label is the name on the tape.
- Shell picks smoke, cream, red, or blue.
- Tape sets the length, 30 minutes a side for a C60 or 45 for a C90.
- Meters turns the level meters on or off.
