# HiFi VFD Spectrum

A vacuum fluorescent spectrum that moves to your music's beat.

Sixteen phosphor bars move to the playing song's beat and rest while it is paused. When nothing is playing, they run a demo.

## Settings

- Style: Bars or Peaks.
- Colors: Teal, Amber, or Cobalt.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
