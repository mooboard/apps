# Minesweeper

Clear a minefield cell by cell, guided by the numbers.

Ten mines hide in a 16 by 4 field, and the first cell is always safe. The board solves it from the clues and guesses when it must. Play it yourself with the game pad on your phone. A opens a cell and B plants a flag.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
