# Falling Blocks

Blocks fall, stack, and clear lines by themselves.

Each piece drops into the best spot the board can find. Beside the well you see the lines cleared and the next piece. When the well fills up, it sweeps clean and starts over.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
