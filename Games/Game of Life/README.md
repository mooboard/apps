# Game of Life

Conway's Game of Life on your mooboard, with soft fading trails.

Cells are born and die by the classic rules. When the board dies out or settles, it starts again with a new seed.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal or fast.
- Colors: Mint, Ember, Rainbow or White.
- Density: how full the first board is.
- Start again when it settles: on by default.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
