# Tiny Dungeon Crawler

A retro dungeon crawl with monsters and loot.

A knight crosses each dungeon floor to the stairs, opening chests and fighting skeletons, bats, and slimes along the way.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.
- Dungeon: Crypt, Lava Dungeon, or Ice Cavern.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
