# Frogger

Arrows hop through two traffic lanes, a safe median, and two moving-log lanes. Fill five homes to advance. Logs carry the frog; deep water, cars, occupied homes, edges, and the thirty-second timer cost a life.

The game plays itself during its display turn. On boards with game controls, arrows and buttons take over. Three lives; A or B restarts after game over. Autoplay restarts automatically. Choose Slow, Normal, or Fast. Progress lasts for the current display turn.

Requires firmware 0.4.0 carrying the `frogger` renderer. Original compact pixel artwork; no original arcade assets are included.
