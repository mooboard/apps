# Pac-Man

The blue maze spells MOOBOARD. Eat the dots, take a power pellet, and turn the
tables on four ghosts. Doors in the letter shapes connect the inner passages;
the exits on either side join into a wraparound tunnel.

The game plays itself during its display turn. On boards with game controls,
use the arrows to steer. A turn waits for the next open passage. After three
lives, A or B starts again; autoplay restarts on its own. Clear every dot to
advance a level. Score, remaining lives, and level appear above the maze.

Choose Slow, Normal, or Fast. Progress lasts for the current display turn.
Requires firmware with the `pacman` renderer (minimum 0.4.0).

Original MOOBOARD maze and pixel artwork. No Google assets are included.
