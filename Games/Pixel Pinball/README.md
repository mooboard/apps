# Pixel Pinball

Arcade pinball with bumpers, drop targets, and flippers.

A sideways table has three bumpers and two flippers. Dropping all three targets raises the multiplier.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.
- Table: Cyber Neon, Classic Arcade, or Emerald Casino.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
