# Word Guess

Find the five-letter word in six tries.

Green is the right letter in the right spot, and yellow is the right letter in another spot. The board plays practice words on its own. The day's word is yours, with the game pad on your phone.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
