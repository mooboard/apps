# Word Guess

Find the five-letter word in six tries.

Green is the right letter in the right spot, and yellow is the right letter in another spot. The board plays practice words on its own. The day's word is yours, with the game pad on your phone. Your guesses can be any five-letter word in Webster's 1934 dictionary. Offline, the board takes only the game's own words.

## Settings

- Speed: slow, normal, or fast.

It shows for 15 seconds each minute, after the clock. Change its settings in your board's web app.
