# Scoreboard

Two scores you keep from your phone.

Tap plus or minus for each side. Scores go from 0 to 999, and a new score flashes as it changes.

## Settings

- Names: up to 8 letters for each side.
- Colors: one for each side.

It stays on your mooboard until you tap Done, or for 3 hours after the last tap. Keep score in your board's web app.
