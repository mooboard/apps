# Limits

Your Claude Code session and weekly limits as two bars.

The top bar is your 5-hour session with the time until it resets. The bottom bar is your week with the day it resets. They
turn amber at 70% and red at 90%. When Claude Code hasn't sent anything for 15 minutes, the bars dim and show how long
it's been idle.

## Setup

Limits needs Claude Code on a Pro or Max plan and a small script on your computer that Claude Code runs as its status
line. The script sends only the two percentages and their reset times. It never reads or sends your Claude sign-in. At
home it sends straight to your board. To see your limits away from home too, make a key on the app's page in your
board's web app and add it to the script.
