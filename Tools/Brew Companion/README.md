# Brew Companion

Precision coffee brewing and tea steeping companion for your kitchen.

Guides blooms, multistage pours, and infusion times with large-digit countdowns and stage indicators.

## Settings

- Steep: duration for bloom or infusion stage.
- Pour/Rest: rest duration between pours.
- Stages: total brewing phases.

Trigger brews and monitor timers directly from your board's web app.
