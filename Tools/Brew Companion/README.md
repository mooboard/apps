# Brew Companion

Tap a brew and it counts down while the water darkens. A tea bag dunks in the mug, or the French press waits and then presses down. It says when it's ready, with a chime on a board with a speaker.

Nothing leaves your board.

## Brews

- Green tea, 2 min 30 s
- Black tea, 4 min
- Oolong, 3 min
- Herbal tea, 5 min
- French press, 4 min

## Controls

- Each brew starts its timer.
- Stop ends it.
- Time left shows the countdown.
