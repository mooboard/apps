# Chess Clock

Two clocks for chess or any turn-based game.

Tap your side on your phone to pass the turn. Tenths show under 10 seconds, and a side that runs out turns red.

## Settings

- Time: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes.
- Increment: 0, 1, 2, 3, or 5 seconds.

It stays on your mooboard until you tap Done. Run it in your board's web app.
