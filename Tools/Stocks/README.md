# Stocks

Your stocks and coins with today's change, three stocks and then two coins. There's nothing to set up.

In a clock face's editor, pick Stock: AAPL or Coin: BTC for your first stock or coin. It shows the price with a green or red arrow.

## Settings

- Stock 1, Stock 2, and Stock 3, such as AAPL.
- Coin 1 and Coin 2, such as BTC.

Stock prices come through mooboard from Finnhub, and coin prices come from CoinGecko.
