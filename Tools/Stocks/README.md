# Stocks

Your stocks and coins with today's change, three stocks and then two coins.

Stocks need a free key from finnhub.io. Paste it into the Finnhub key setting in your board's web app. Coins need no key.

Add Stock 1 or Coin 1 to a clock face in the face's editor. It shows the price with a green or red arrow.

## Settings

- Finnhub key.
- Stock 1, Stock 2, and Stock 3, such as AAPL.
- Coin 1 and Coin 2, by their CoinGecko names, such as bitcoin.
