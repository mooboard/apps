# Earthquake Seismograph

The world's latest earthquake from the USGS, with no key. Its magnitude shows in a color for its level, with how long ago it was and how far it is from your board. A seismograph pen draws a trace that shakes harder for a bigger quake.

Your place is your board's, the one you picked at setup, and it stays on your board.

## Settings

- Smallest is the smallest magnitude to show, 2.5, 4.5, or 6.
- Distance is in miles or kilometers.
