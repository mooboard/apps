# Focus

Focus for 25 minutes, then take a break.

A Pomodoro timer on your whole mooboard. Four dots count the focus blocks in a set, and a bar fills as each phase runs. Every fourth break can be a long one.

## Settings

- Focus: 15, 20, 25, 30, 45, or 50 minutes.
- Break: 3, 5, or 10 minutes.
- Long break every fourth: off, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.

It stays on your mooboard while it runs. Start, pause, skip, and stop it in your board's web app.
